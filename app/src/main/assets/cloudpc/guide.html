3. Install apps

  1. Open App Store (desktop icon or Start menu). Groups on the left: Popular, AI & Coding, Office, Photo & Video, Games, Web apps, Installed.
  2. Tap Install on an app. The card and the bar at the bottom show the progress. You can queue more apps – they install one after another.
  3. When it is done the card shows ✓ Installed with Open and Remove – it cannot be downloaded twice by mistake.
  4. New apps get an icon on the desktop (games in the Games folder) and appear in the Start menu.
  5. For anything else use the search box (60,000+ Ubuntu apps), for example chess, pdf or music.

Web apps (no download)

YouTube, Gmail, Google Docs, WhatsApp, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, GitHub and Canva open as their own windows. Tap Add – the icon appears in the Web Apps folder on the desktop and in the Start menu.

Games

Minetest (Minecraft-like), car games SuperTuxKart, Trigger Rally and TORCS, plus Neverball, Tux Racer, OpenArena and the Games pack (SuperTux, X-Moto, 2048, Chess, Solitaire, OpenTTD…). Games show an FPS counter in the top left corner and are held at a steady 60 FPS at most. Right Shift+F12 hides the counter. Choose AirBoost → Game and set graphics to Low inside 3D games.

Apps from a website (.deb)

Download the ARM64 (aarch64) .deb file in Firefox (for example VS Code from code.visualstudio.com) and tap it in the Downloads folder. It installs, gets a desktop icon and works like an App Store app.

If an app does not open, AirCursor PC tries again with safe settings and then shows what went wrong. Big apps (LibreOffice, GIMP, VS Code, Blender) need up to a minute the first time – an "Opening…" message appears.
Windows programs (.exe) do not run here – this is Linux. Most popular programs have a Linux version in the App Store.

4. AI apps

Claude, ChatGPT, GeminiOpen the AI Apps folder on the desktop. They open in Firefox – sign in once with your own account and Firefox remembers it.
PC Assistant (Claude)AI Apps → PC Assistant. Claude checks the PC (pc-doctor), looks at the screen, fixes speed, FPS and app problems, installs apps, builds projects and opens them on the desktop. It asks before deleting anything. Claude account needed (Claude Code from the App Store).
Offline AIWorks without internet using a small model (Ollama). First start downloads about 1 GB. It is slower on a phone.
Claude CodeAI that writes code in the Terminal. Install it from the App Store, then open AI Apps → Claude Code. Sign-in opens in your phone's own browser (fast, Google sign-in works). After signing in, come back to AirCursor PC – it continues by itself. If it asks for a code: copy it on the phone, then toolbar → Paste and Enter.

Your own private PC

  • Every phone has its own AirCursor PC. It lives in the app's private storage on that phone – there is no shared server. Your apps, projects, websites you build and files stay on your phone; no other AirCursor user can see them, and nothing of theirs comes into yours.
  • Other apps on the phone cannot read the PC either (Android keeps every app's storage private), and the screen goes through a private connection inside the app, not the network.
  • Password, like Windows: the first time you open the PC it offers to set one (or tap Skip; tick Don't show me again to stop the question). After that the PC asks for it on its wallpaper while it starts behind – no extra waiting.
  • Set, change or remove the password any time: toolbar → Lock. "Lock now" locks at once; the PC also locks itself when you were away for 2 minutes.
  • Forgot it? Tap Forgot password? and confirm with your phone's own screen lock, then set a new one. Your files stay.
  • Removing AirCursor PC (or the app) deletes the PC and everything in it – save important files to the phone or GitHub/Drive first.

Smooth videos and speed

  • Air Store (dock or desktop): apps like an iPhone store. Web apps – Spotify, YouTube Music, JioSaavn, Netflix, JioHotstar, Instagram, Photopea, Figma, Notion, AI assistants – are added in a second and open in Air. PC apps (VS Code, GIMP, LibreOffice, games…) install inside the PC with a progress ring. "Search all Ubuntu apps" finds more.
  • PC style: modern dock at the bottom (default) or the Windows taskbar – toolbar → lightning → PC style. Wallpaper: toolbar → picture button.
  • Games run at the Game resolution (960 wide) while they are open – much smoother – and the desktop goes back afterwards.
  • Air – AirCursor's browser: the PC's default browser. It runs on the phone's own engine (Mozilla GeckoView), shown over the PC, so pages, YouTube and AI apps (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) are as fast as on the phone – and Google sign-in works. Open it from the Air desktop icon, the toolbar (🌐) or any link in the PC. Tabs, address bar, Desktop site, back key; ▢ = window size, ⌄ = minimize (tabs stay), ✕ = close. Downloads go to the PC's Downloads folder; uploads can come from the PC or the phone. Brave stays for extensions and web developer tools (toolbar → lightning → Web links).
  • Smooth video: open the video in Brave or Firefox, then press Win+Y (or toolbar → ▶). It pauses in the PC and continues at the same second in a small window over the PC, played by the phone's own video chip – smooth like the YouTube app. Drag the window by its top bar, make it bigger, or close it.
  • Video files in the PC: right-click → Play smoothly on the phone.
  • Copy a YouTube link in the PC and a Play smoothly button appears at the top.
  • Focus boost: the app you are using gets the phone's fast processor cores; apps in the background use the power-saving cores.

5. Files

  • Your files are in the Home folder (Documents, Downloads, Pictures…).
  • Bring files from your phone: toolbar → Files → Import from phone. They are copied into Downloads/From phone.
  • Send files to your phone: put them in the To phone folder on the desktop, then toolbar → Files → Save to phone.
  • Files you import are copies – the originals on your phone are never changed or deleted.
Everything in AirCursor PC is stored inside the AirCursor app. Uninstalling AirCursor deletes it – save important files to your phone first. App updates keep everything.

Sound

Sound from videos, music and games plays on the phone's speaker or headphones. Change the volume with the phone's volume buttons. Volume per app: Start → PulseAudio Volume Control.

Projects, GitHub and Google Drive

  • Projects folder in Home: Claude Code and the PC Assistant start there. The Files sidebar also has Documents, Downloads, Google Drive, To phone and From phone.
  • Send to phone: in Files, right-click a file or folder → Send to phone → choose where to save it. Folders are zipped first.
  • GitHub (desktop icon): sign in on your phone, then clone a repository, publish a folder as a new repository, save your changes (push) or get the latest (pull).
  • Google Drive (desktop icon): sign in on your phone; the Google Drive folder in Home syncs both ways with the folder "AirCursor PC" in your Drive. All other Drive files: "Open drive.google.com".
Files inside AirCursor PC are deleted if you uninstall AirCursor. Keep important projects on GitHub, Google Drive or your phone.

6. Internet – Air

Air is AirCursor's own browser. It runs on the phone's engine over the PC, so YouTube, AI chats and big sites are fast, and Google sign-in works. Pages look like on a laptop (the same size as the PC), with the desktop version of YouTube.

  • New tab: real photos of the Earth (NASA), a clock, your most-visited sites with their real logos, and a search box with a mic.
  • Shields (the orange shield): trackers and ads are blocked; the number shows how many on this page. Tap it to turn Shields off for one site.
  • Bookmarks: ☆ in the address bar (Ctrl+D). History: Ctrl+H. Downloads: Ctrl+J.
  • Downloads go into the PC (Downloads folder), onto the phone (Downloads/AirCursor), or both – Air asks, or set "always" in Air → Settings. Handy for projects you build with Claude.
  • Keys: Ctrl+T new tab, Ctrl+W close, Ctrl+Tab next tab, Ctrl+L address bar, Ctrl+F find, Ctrl+Shift+N private tab, F11 window size.
  • App windows: Air Store, TS Station, YouTube and web apps open as their own windows without an address bar. Drag the title bar to move, the corner to resize, double-tap the title bar to maximize. The 🌐 toolbar button lists all windows.

Prefer the PC's own browser (Brave or Firefox) for web links? AirCursor toolbar → ⚡ AirBoost → Web links.

Security

The whole PC runs inside the AirCursor app's own sandbox from Android: it cannot see your phone's other apps, photos or messages. Because of that, Firefox's extra Linux sandbox is not needed (and cannot run inside an Android app). Firefox here opens websites with HTTPS only, blocks trackers and gets security updates automatically in the background.

7. Keyboard and copy-paste

  • Toolbar → keyboard button shows the phone keyboard.
  • Bluetooth or USB keyboard and mouse work like on a PC: all keys and shortcuts, right click, middle click and the scroll wheel.
  • Game controller (Bluetooth or USB, connected to the phone): D-pad or left stick = arrow keys, A = Enter, B = Esc, X = Space, Y = F, L1/R1 = Q/E, L2/R2 = Z/X, Start = Menu, Select = F1. Buttons stay pressed as long as you hold them, so games can be steered and driven with it.
  • Ctrl, Alt and Win in the toolbar stay pressed for the next key (for example Ctrl, then C).
  • Text you copy in the PC is automatically copied to your phone.
  • To paste text from your phone into the PC, use toolbar → Paste.
  • Big files from the phone (photos, videos, PDFs): in any phone app tap Share → AirCursor PC. They land in Downloads → From phone, much faster than uploading inside the PC.

8. Speed (AirBoost)

ProfileUse it for
Turbo3D games on mid-range phones – most FPS (800 px)
GameGames and videos – high FPS
FastFast and smooth everyday use on slower phones
BalancedRecommended for most phones
QualitySharpest text on fast phones

Change the profile any time – it applies at once: toolbar → AirBoost (lightning button), or AirCursor → AirCursor PC. AirBoost draws the desktop with fewer pixels and sharpens it on the phone (an FSR-style upscaler, like DLSS), so it stays clear but runs faster. Boost now stops background jobs; the FPS button shows the frame rate.

Graphics: games are drawn by a virtual graphics card that uses every CPU core of the phone (llvmpipe). Open Benchmark on the desktop to measure the FPS, change the profile and compare.

Graphics for games: AirBoost → Graphics → CPU or Phone GPU (VirGL). Phone GPU lets games draw on the phone's graphics chip. Depending on the game it is faster or slower than the CPU – open Benchmark: it measures both. Games use the choice from their next start; if a game looks wrong, switch back to CPU.

Steady FPS: toolbar → AirBoost → Steady FPS (or AirCursor → AirCursor PC). Games and the desktop are held at 30, 45 or 60 FPS. A steady 30 or 45 feels smoother than jumping between 15 and 90, and the phone stays cooler, so it does not slow down after a few minutes. For 3D games choose 30 or 45; turning hand control off while playing also frees the CPU.

Reading the FPS meter: the PC only sends the screen when something changes. A still screen therefore shows Idle – that is not lag. Watch the number while you scroll, play or watch a video. Videos themselves are usually 25–30 FPS.

Storage

AirCursor PC has no fixed size: it can use all the free space of your phone (the App Store shows it at the bottom left). For more space use Google Drive from the App Store (15 GB free), or move files to the phone with Toolbar → Files.
